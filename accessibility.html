Accessibility Statement

Spurs.gov is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people with disabilities.

Our Commitment

We are continually improving the user experience for everyone and applying the relevant accessibility standards to ensure we provide equal access to all of our users.

Standards Compliance

This website strives to conform to Level AA of the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C) Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1. These guidelines explain how to make web content more accessible for people with disabilities.

Conformance with these guidelines will help make the web more user-friendly for all people, including:

  • People who are blind or have low vision
  • People who are deaf or hard of hearing
  • People with limited mobility or dexterity
  • People with cognitive disabilities
  • People using assistive technologies

Section 508 Compliance

As a U.S. government website, we adhere to Section 508 of the Rehabilitation Act, which requires federal agencies to make their electronic and information technology accessible to people with disabilities.

Accessibility Features

This website includes the following accessibility features:

  • Semantic HTML markup for proper content structure
  • Alternative text for images
  • Keyboard navigation support
  • ARIA landmarks and labels
  • Clear and consistent navigation
  • Sufficient color contrast
  • Resizable text without loss of functionality
  • Skip navigation links
  • Form labels and instructions

Known Issues

We are aware that some parts of this website are not fully accessible:

  • Some older PDF documents may not be fully accessible
  • Some third-party content may not be fully compliant

We are working to address these issues and improve accessibility across all content.

Assistive Technologies

This website is designed to be compatible with:

  • Screen readers (JAWS, NVDA, VoiceOver)
  • Screen magnifiers
  • Speech recognition software
  • Alternative input devices

Browser Compatibility

This website is tested with current versions of:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Apple Safari
  • Microsoft Edge
